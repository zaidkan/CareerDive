# CareerDive Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 12, 2026**

CareerDive is a free, non-commercial educational project created and operated by **Zaid Khan as an individual**. It is not a company, and it does not earn money from your use of the site.

I want CareerDive to collect only the information it needs to run a career simulation, remember what happened, and help when something goes wrong. This policy explains what is collected, what it is used for, and how to contact me.

## 1. Who CareerDive is for

CareerDive is intended for people **age 13 or older**. It is not directed at children under 13. By clicking **Start**, you confirm that you are at least 13 and agree to this Privacy Policy and the [Terms of Use](TERMS.md). CareerDive does not verify your age.

**If you are under 13, please do not use CareerDive.** If you or a parent or guardian believes a child under 13 has used the simulation, email [khn.zaid.2008@gmail.com](mailto:khn.zaid.2008@gmail.com) so I can help locate and remove the associated information.

CareerDive is currently for individual exploration. School or institutional accounts and deployments are not enabled. The project is not represented as FERPA- or COPPA-certified.

## 2. What CareerDive collects

**Anonymous session identity.** You do not need to sign up, create a user-facing account, log in with a name, or provide an email address to use the simulation. Behind the scenes, Supabase anonymous authentication assigns your browser a random user identifier and an authentication session. This helps keep one visitor's saved information separate from another visitor's information. Your browser stores information needed to maintain that session.

An anonymous identifier does **not** make someone impossible to recognize or trace. It is still a persistent technical identifier associated with saved simulation activity.

**Simulation information.** CareerDive saves your simulation session and progress, the actions and choices you make, system-generated observations about what happened, and any closed-ended reflection choices you submit. There is no free-text reflection entry at launch.

**Optional problem reports.** If you choose to send a **Report a problem** submission, CareerDive collects the description and category you provide. You can also choose to include contact information, such as your email address, and optional technical details to help diagnose a problem. The technical details can include information such as browser version, graphics support, screen size, motion preference, and the page or error context. Please avoid including names, addresses, school information, or other private details in the description unless truly necessary.

**Basic network and operational information.** When you visit CareerDive, the services that host and run it may process information needed to deliver requests and keep the service working, such as IP addresses, request details, and technical or security logs. Not providing a name or email for gameplay does not prevent this ordinary network processing.

## 3. Why this information is used

This information is used to:

- Run the simulation and save or resume your progress.
- Keep your session information separate from other visitors' information.
- Show results and reflections based on your choices and the simulation's rules.
- Investigate problems and respond when you voluntarily provide contact information.
- Maintain the service, troubleshoot errors, and help protect it from abuse.

CareerDive does **not** sell personal information, build advertising profiles, run cross-site tracking, or use behavioral advertising.

## 4. Analytics, cookies, and browser storage

**CareerDive collects a small number of anonymous, aggregate usage counts.** These are first-party counts that measure how many times basic milestones happen — for example, that someone opened the site, viewed a career, or started or completed a shift. Each count is stored as a random one-time identifier, the name of the milestone, and the time it happened. These records contain **no names, no email addresses, no free text, no IP addresses, no device fingerprints, and no link to your anonymous session identity**. They cannot be used to recognize you, and they are only used in aggregate to understand overall usage of the project.

CareerDive does **not** use any third-party analytics service, session replay, heatmaps, keystroke recording, cross-site tracking, or tracking cookies.

CareerDive does use browser storage for necessary functions, such as keeping an anonymous authentication session and remembering certain simulation or interface state. This is different from using cookies to track you across websites.

If I later add a third-party analytics service or collect anything beyond these anonymous aggregate counts, I will review the information collected and update this policy before enabling it, including any choices or notices required by applicable law.

## 5. Services that help run CareerDive

CareerDive uses the following service providers:

- **Render** hosts the website and backend service. It may process ordinary request, network, and operational information, including IP addresses and logs.
- **Supabase** provides the database and anonymous authentication. It processes the session identifier, authentication information, and information stored by the simulation.

## 6. How long information is kept

**Saved simulation sessions older than 90 days are automatically deleted**, measured from when each session was created. Updating or resuming a shift does not restart that period. Database-backed servers run cleanup at startup and hourly; outages, active writes, or a large backlog can delay deletion. The cleanup includes associated activity, observations, and reflections.

The 90-day simulation-data rule does not necessarily apply to optional support reports, emails sent to me, anonymous authentication records, or network/security logs maintained by service providers. These are handled separately for support and operational purposes. You may contact me to request deletion of information associated with your use of CareerDive.

Clearing your browser storage may remove your ability to return to an anonymous session, but it does not by itself delete records already stored on the backend.
