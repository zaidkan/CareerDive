# Phase 1.5 Validation Gate

Decision must be recorded before adding second career or 3D navigation.

## Measures

- Career understanding versus high-quality static material.
- Decision usefulness and confidence calibration.
- Practitioner realism and stereotype review.
- Debrief claim reliability.
- Accessible-mode conclusion parity.
- Gaming and interface-skill confounds.
- Authoring hours and maintenance estimate.
- Median, p95, and maximum session cost.
- NPC leakage, contradiction, and false-block rates.

## Precommitted outcomes

- **Proceed:** Understanding and usefulness improve, practitioner review passes, accessibility conclusions remain comparable, and economics fit target.
- **Proceed with narrower claims:** Experience is useful, but evidence or debrief claims require reduction.
- **Redesign and retest:** Core value appears plausible while interface, content, or AI controls cause failures.
- **Pivot:** Immersion adds no material decision value over simpler materials.
- **Halt:** Product repeatedly misleads users, fails privacy/safety requirements, or has no meaningful advantage.

Enjoyment alone cannot trigger “Proceed.”
